# ForgeLaunch.ai — Client Acquisition Pipeline

## Positioning
ForgeLaunch.ai builds practical digital products and systems for startups and growing businesses.

Core offers:
1. Web & Business Applications
2. MVP & Product Development
3. AI & Workflow Automation
4. Custom Internal Tools, Dashboards & Portals
5. Ongoing Product Support

Additional specialist services:
- Supply Chain Solutions
- Procurement Solutions

Supply chain and procurement are capabilities within the broader offer, not the primary market positioning.

## Ideal prospect signals
Prioritize organizations showing one or more of these signals:
- launching or validating a new digital product
- manual spreadsheet/email/WhatsApp workflows
- repetitive admin or data-entry work
- disconnected tools or reporting
- need for customer/vendor/staff portal
- need for an internal dashboard or business application
- AI feature or automation opportunity with a clear business workflow
- outdated or inadequate customer-facing web experience
- procurement/supply-chain workflow issue (specialist segment)

## Prospect segments
- Startups / founders: MVPs, product development, AI features
- Service businesses: lead, operations and workflow systems
- SMBs / growing businesses: internal apps, portals, dashboards, automation
- Professional services: workflow automation, client portals, reporting
- E-commerce / distribution: operational tools, integrations, dashboards
- Manufacturing / operations: internal systems and workflow automation
- Supply chain / procurement teams: specialist workflows when relevant

## Qualification
Capture:
- Company
- Website
- Contact
- Role
- Segment

- Problem hypothesis
- Best ForgeLaunch offer
- Personalization note
- Contact channel
- First contact date
- Follow-up date
- Status
- Reply summary
- Next action
- Scope / budget / timeline notes

## Pipeline stages
Research → Qualified → Ready for outreach → Contacted → Follow-up 1 → Follow-up 2 → Replied → Discovery → Proposal → Negotiation → Won / Lost / Nurture

## Outreach rules
- Research before contacting.
- Personalize around a visible business/product/workflow signal.
- Do not invent pain points; frame uncertain observations as questions.
- Do not claim client results we do not have.
- Use FlowPilot only as a portfolio demonstration.
- Keep the first message short and problem-focused.
- No binding price, scope or delivery commitment without approval.

## Discovery checklist
Problem → current process → users → desired outcome → must-have features → integrations/data → constraints → timeline → budget context → success criteria.

## Proposal structure
Context → objective → scope → deliverables → assumptions → exclusions → timeline → milestones → price/payment → client responsibilities → change process → support/maintenance → next step.

## Activity target
Build toward 20 well-researched prospects/day, 20 personalized first contacts/day and 5 follow-ups/day. These are operating targets, not promised results.
